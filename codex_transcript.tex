\begin{abstract}
This paper outlines a multistage, zero-shot Natural Language Inference (NLI) architectural approach towards large scale data classification. By moving beyond traditional horizontal scaling and extrinsic bias metadata, we propose a vertical pipeline built on Homotopy Type Theory and the Johnson-Lindenstrauss lemma. Through empirical telemetry and topological diagnostics, we demonstrate that semantic structure is robust but intrinsically observer-dependent, effectively disproving the fiction of objective neutrality in automated media analysis.
