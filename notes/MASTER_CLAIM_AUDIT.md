# Master Claim Audit (Updated September 2026)

## Scope and Purpose

This document provides a definitive, audited accounting of all methodological and empirical claims developed across the Belief Transformer project. It formalizes claim boundaries, evidential status, and dispositions following exhaustive combinatorial testing, non-parametric permutation gating, and code-level provenance audits.

## Claim Taxonomy

- **VALIDATED**: Supported by executable scripts, hash-bound artifacts, exact or non-parametric permutation nulls, and successful out-of-sample or cross-model replication.
- **CORPUS-DEPENDENT / QUALIFIED**: Statistically significant in specific corpus/hypothesis configurations, but boundary conditions restrict universal generalization.
- **MODEL-DEPENDENT / QUALIFIED**: Replicates under certain model architectures/pretraining objectives but fails or attenuates under others.
- **OPERATOR-DEPENDENT / QUALIFIED**: Detected properties vary systematically with the mathematical measurement operator applied to probe responses.
- **SUGGESTIVE / NON-DECISIVE**: Demonstrates nominal association or sensitivity under specific subsets, but fails primary preregistered thresholds or full-sample robustness.
- **PRELIMINARY NEGATIVE / UNRESOLVED**: Initial tests yielded negative or near-zero effects, but the condition was inadequately controlled or lacked multi-seed ablations; cannot be classified as definitively killed.
- **QUARANTINED — NO INFERENCE PERMITTED**: Mathematically invalid or uncalibrated calculations (e.g., reversed composition, non-independent dyad testing, missing null models) from which zero empirical or theoretical inferences may be drawn.
- **DEMOTED AS UNNECESSARY MACHINERY**: Complex algorithmic constructs (e.g., random initializations as "observers", kernel expansions as "perspectives") that add no measurable value over simpler, directly authored representations.

## Foundational Epistemological Realignment

### The Six Core Principles of Observer-Conditioned Measurement
1. **Authored contrastive measurements can exhibit non-random structure.** Exhaustive combinatorial testing confirms that authored contrastive pairs align in transformer representation space far beyond chance ($p = 7.61 \times 10^{-5}$ on $N=60$, $p = 0.00115$ on fresh $N=120$).
2. **Article-level probe responses can form statistically detectable latent dimensions.** Projections of articles onto contrastive margin vectors compress into low-dimensional subspaces that survive publisher-block permutation nulls ($p < 0.0001$).
3. **The number of detectable dimensions is not universally one.** While Israel--Palestine under paired displacement collapses to $k=1$, the Russia--Ukraine war corpus yields a verified rank of $k=2$ ($85.45\%$ cumulative variance, both $p < 0.001$).
4. **Detected dimensionality is conditional on the measurement regime.** Geometric rank is a function of the joint measurement system:
   $$\mathcal{G}_{\text{observed}} = f(\text{corpus},\; \text{hypothesis bank},\; \text{measurement operator},\; \text{NLI model})$$
   It shifts with corpus structure, probe definitions, operator choice ($s_A$ vs $s_A - s_B$), and encoder architecture.
5. **Verdict dimensionality and hidden-state axis coherence are separate, dissociable constructs.** Low-dimensional article response covariance does not entail high-dimensional representation alignment, as empirically demonstrated by Ukraine ($k_{\text{verdict}} = 2$ at $p < 0.001$ vs axis coherence $p = 0.430$).
6. **Controlled positive experiments verify apparatus resolving power.** Synthetic factorial experiments confirm that the pipeline resolves $k=2$ when two orthogonal factors exist ($99.11\%$) and collapses to $k=1$ when only one is probed ($97.48\%$), ruling out mechanical collapse defects.

### Methodological Safeguards
- **Construct Validity Over Metaphysical Claims**: The architecture tests construct validity under declared observer frames, not the "relativity of truth" or "impossibility of neutrality."
- **Structured Perspectivism**: Effective rank is constrained but not fixed. Observer conditioning reveals bounded, measurable regularities rather than an incommensurable grab-bag of perceptions.
- **The Roll-Call Precaution**: Low-dimensional compression is an empirical convergence phenomenon shared with legislative roll-call models (e.g., NOMINATE). Geometric similarity does not establish identical political or behavioral mechanisms.
- **Reconstruction Discipline**: *Once a target number is known, reconstruction risks becoming optimization toward the remembered result.* All historical exploratory branches, mathematical flaws, and negative results are preserved as explicit boundary conditions.
