\begin{abstract}
This paper develops a multistage, zero-shot Natural Language Inference architecture for large-scale classification using Riemannian tools to formalize semantic geometry. It examines the ``geometric fiction of objective neutrality'': the reification of a frame-dependent coordinate, metric, and aggregation rule as an unmarked center of political discourse. Ground News provides the principal case of horizontal expansion through publisher-level, exogenous bias metadata. In response, the paper proposes a vertical, content-conditioned and observer-indexed program informed by topos theory and Homotopy Type Theory, without claiming that either formalism automatically supplies an NLP algorithm. The Johnson--Lindenstrauss lemma motivates dimensional adequacy by making the distortion cost of flattening a finite semantic configuration mathematically explicit. The resulting framework treats disagreement as structured evidence while retaining factual and journalistic constraints.
\end{abstract}
